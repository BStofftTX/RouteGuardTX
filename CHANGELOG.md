# Changelog

All notable changes to RouteGuardTX are documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Formal release-change record for the offline demonstration program.
- Release-readiness evidence separating demonstrated planning behavior from authoritative and safety limitations.
- Browser proof that offline results remain labeled as synthetic and custom constraints are not transferred to consumer map links.

### Changed

- Updated ESLint to 10.15.0 to remove its vulnerable minimatch dependency path.
- Allowed the local Playwright preview port to be selected with `ROUTEGUARD_E2E_PORT` while preserving the CI default.

### Safety

- RouteGuardTX remains a planning demonstration. It does not provide verified
  navigation, legal restriction, clearance, bridge-capacity, or safety determinations.
